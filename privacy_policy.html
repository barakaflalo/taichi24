Privacy Policy — Tai Chi 24

Everything stays on your device. All your data (progress, preferences, username, backups) is stored locally in your browser only. Nothing is sent to an AppNest server — we don't run one that collects data.

What leaves the device, and when: images and movement frames load from the static site (GitHub Pages), with no tracking. The optional AI assistant only sends your question text directly to the provider you connect (Gemini / Claude / OpenAI / local) using your own key (BYOK); the key is stored on your device and never included in backups.

No tracking, analytics, ads, or third-party cookies. You can back up, restore, and erase all data anytime from Settings.

This app is a general tai chi learning tool, not medical advice. Consult a qualified instructor and/or physician before physical activity. Use at your own risk.
